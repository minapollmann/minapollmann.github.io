Dr. Mina Pollmann was most recently a Presidential Management Fellow with the U.S. Department of State's Office of Japanese Affairs. Previously, she was an Associate Political Scientist at <a href="https://www.rand.org/pubs/authors/p/pollmann_mina.html">the RAND Corporation</a>. She has also held visiting research appointments with the Harvard Kennedy School and University of Tokyo, and worked as a News Producer for the Washington Bureau of TV Tokyo. She holds a Ph.D. in Political Science from MIT, and a B.S. in Foreign Service from Georgetown University. Her dissertation was on <a href="https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/152841?show=full">the Political Origins of Alliances</a> using historical cases from Japan and graduate studies were funded by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship. Her research interests are Japan’s security and diplomacy, the U.S.-Japan alliance, alliance politics, and Indo-Pacific security. She writes on these topics regularly for <a href="https://thediplomat.com/authors/mina-pollmann/">The Diplomat</a>. 

She is based in Haarlem, the Netherlands, and looking for Japan- and security-related research roles.
