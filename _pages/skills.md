### Qualitative Research

As part of obtaining my PhD in political science from MIT, I was trained in qualitative and quantitative research design. I have the tools to answer complex questions, such as developing appropriate case selection and measurement strategies, and through my <a href="https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/152841?show=full">dissertation research</a>, my <a href="https://www.rand.org/pubs/authors/p/pollmann_mina.html">research for the RAND Corporation</a>, and my <a href="https://thediplomat.com/authors/mina-pollmann/">writings for <i>The Diplomat</i></a>, I have gained extensive experience conducting archival research and interviews with practitioners and subject matter experts. I am excellent at finding multiple different sources of data to answer my research questions, including but not limited to: national and local government reports, contemporary news coverage, and politicians' social media. I have also conducted research at the U.S. Library of Congress in Washington, DC, and the Japanese National Diet Library in Tokyo. I use English and Japanese primary and secondary sources as well as interviews in English and Japanese in my research. Furthermore, I have honed my writing skills to be able to effectively communicate the conclusions of my research and analysis. 

### Public Engagement

I have extensive experience with public engagement including: giving talks and lectures; writing <a href="https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/10357718.2015.1034088?journalCode=caji20">peer-reviewed articles</a>, <a href="https://www.csis.org/analysis/resolved-japan-has-not-done-enough-bolster-immigration">op-eds</a>, <a href="https://www.eastwestcenter.org/publications/opening-australias-black-box-the-domestic-debate-over-submarine-production">think tank pieces</a>, and <a href="https://www.h-net.org/reviews/showpdf.php?id=56850">book reviews</a>; <a href="https://cimsec.org/sea-control-130-stephen-biddle-future-warfare-western-pacific/">hosting</a> and <a href="https://thediplomat.com/2016/06/abenomics-the-upper-house-elections-and-japans-political-future/?utm_content=buffer913c2&utm_medium=social&utm_source=twitter.com&utm_campaign=buffer">speaking</a> on podcasts; and moderating public panels. I have given multiple talks at MIT; in addition, I have given talks at <a href="https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/events/2025/11/japans-iron-lady-sanae-takaichi-and-the-new-japanese-government">Leiden University</a>, the <a href="https://www.eui.eu/events?id=578326">European Union Institute</a>, University of Tokyo, <a href="https://macmillan.yale.edu/events/2022-02/why-risk-alliance-evidence-japan-1901-1960">Yale University</a>, <a href="https://www.belfercenter.org/event/despite-divisions-when-alliances-require-coercion-form">Harvard Kennedy School</a>, and the Horizons in Japanese Politics and Foreign Policy Workshop hosted by Columbia University. I gave a guest lecture on Japanese politics and foreign policy to U.S. diplomats, and guest lectured at Georgetown University and University of Amsterdam. I have the skills to disseminate my research findings to and engage in contemporary debates with the public through many different mediums.
