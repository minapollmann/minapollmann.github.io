### Qualitative Research

As part of obtaining my PhD in political science from MIT, I was trained in qualitative research design. I have the tools to answer complex questions, such as developing appropriate case selection and measurement strategies, and through my <a href="https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/152841?show=full">dissertation research</a>, my <a href="https://www.rand.org/pubs/authors/p/pollmann_mina.html">research for the RAND Corporation</a>, and my <a href="https://thediplomat.com/authors/mina-pollmann/">writings for <i>The Diplomat</i></a>, I have gained extensive experience conducting archival research and interviews with practitioners and subject matter experts. I am excellent at finding multiple different sources of data to answer my research questions, including politicians' personal webpages, Tweets, government reports, and digital newspaper archives and contemporary news coverage. I have also conducted research at the U.S. Library of Congress in Washington, DC, and the Japanese National Diet Library in Tokyo. I use English and Japanese primary and secondary sources as well as interviews in English and Japanese in my research.

### Public Engagement

I have extensive experience with public engagement: including giving talks and lectures; writing peer-reviewed articles, op-eds, think pieces, and book reviews; hosting and speaking on podcasts; and moderating public panels. I have given multiple talks at MIT, and also at <a href="https://ceas.yale.edu/events/why-risk-alliance-evidence-japan-1901-1960">Yale University</a> and the <a href="https://tcjs.u-tokyo.ac.jp/archives/1373">University of Tokyo</a>.
