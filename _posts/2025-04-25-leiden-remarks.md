On April 25, I had the honor of sharing my thoughts on alliances in this uncertain era as a young scholar at the public symposium on <a href="https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/hjd/news/2025/symposium-japans-diplomacy-between-the-east-and-the-west">Japan's Diplomacy between the East and West</a> hosted by the Leiden University Institute of Security and Global Affairs, The Hague Journal of Diplomacy, and the LeidenAsiaCentre.

I focused on three key messages. To **Japan**, I reiterated the importance of building up Japan's own defense capabilities. To **European democracies**, I emphasized the importance of working together more effectively in the security realm. To **international relations scholars**, I advocated for a renewed focus on studying the domestic political foundations of alliance policymaking.
