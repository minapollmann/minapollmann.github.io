I am excited to announce that one of the reports I spent the most time on during my year at the RAND Corporation - <a href="https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA739-8.html??cutoff=true&utm_source=AdaptiveMailer&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=701QK00000SPi7SYAT&utm_term=00vQK00000PFdSVYA1&org=1674&lvl=100&ite=298447&lea=5187263&ctr=0&par=1&trk=a0wQK00000DPw9NYAT">Anticipating Allies’ Responses to U.S. Retrenchment: Lessons from Limited Military Withdrawals During the Cold War</a> - was published today. I would like to thank the PI and co-authors for the incredible learning opportunity and collaborative experience. 

This report is a good example of how I work to use historical evidence to test theories with important policy implications. I also brought my Japanese language skills to bear in both the Japan and Taiwan chapters.
