Today is my first day as the Japan Research Fellow with the <a href="https://leidenasiacentre.nl/people/mina-pollmann/">Leiden Asia Centre</a>! I am very excited for this incredible opportunity.

For the next six months, I will be researching how 32 local cities across 8 prefectures that the Government of Japan has designated as destinations for <a href="https://www.kokuminhogo.go.jp/kunren/ukeire/index.html">Japanese Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) from the Sakishima Islands in a hypothetical Taiwan Strait contingency</a> can better prepare for such a scenario.
